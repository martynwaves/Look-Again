Look Again sound effects: where each file comes from.
All source recordings are CC0 1.0 (public domain): free to use, change and ship, commercially too, with no credit required.
They were taken from the GitHub collection https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds, which gathers:
  Kenney.nl packs (RPG Audio, Impact Sounds, Sci-fi Sounds, Voiceover Pack), Ben Burnes / Abstractionauts sample packs and micro packs
  (Toolbox Rummaging, Keyboard Sounds, Smol Mechanisms, Books Paper Writing, Retail Therapy, Novice Cello, Chairmat, Record Fuzzies,
  NazdyNate Electromagnetic Sounds), and the "100 CC0 SFX" / "30 CC0 SFX loops" packs.
Every clip was trimmed, levelled and turned into Ogg Vorbis; loops were crossfaded so they repeat without a seam.
